    04About

    A video editor with a media buyer's eye.

    I'm a Social Media Video Editor and Creative Video Producer with experience across agency projects, freelance productions and independent creative work. I specialize in DTC advertising, UGC-style content, short-form social media videos and AI-assisted creative production.

    My approach goes beyond editing. I enjoy contributing to creative ideas, developing hooks, selecting footage and transforming concepts into engaging videos.

    Before editing full-time, I ran my own ecommerce business and Meta ad campaigns, so I read CTR and CPA when judging which hooks to keep, not just how a video looks.

    I'm currently looking to collaborate with international creative agencies, brands and content teams through full-time remote opportunities.

    How I work

    1. Brief & hookI get clear on the product, the audience and the promise, then look for the opening that stops the scroll.
    2. Footage selectionI pick the strongest moments and the proof shots. A good edit starts with a good selection.
    3. Edit & pacingCuts, captions, sound and hook variations, with feedback applied quickly.
    4. DeliveryPlatform-ready files in 9:16, 4:5 and 1:1, organised and on deadline.
